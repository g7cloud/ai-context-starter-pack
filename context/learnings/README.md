# Learnings

Lessons, preferences, corrections and useful facts: the things a new session would otherwise get wrong again.
Up: [the map](../README.md).

- **One line for each, newest first:** the date, the lesson, and why in a few words.
- **A lesson that needs explaining** gets its own file here (from `../templates/learning.md`), linked from its line.
- **A rule that must never be broken** belongs in the map's rules, not here.
- **Keep this list under 40 lines.** Past that, move older lessons into the documents they belong to and take
  their lines out.
